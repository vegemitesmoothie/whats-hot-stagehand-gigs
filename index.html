How this list works

v1 · List + links only

Hot = gig date
A call is Hot while its gig date hasn't passed yet — not for 72 hours after it was posted. A gig posted days ago for tomorrow is still Hot; a gig posted this morning for yesterday is already dead. Hot is sorted by gig date, soonest first.
Filled
When a call fills, it leaves Hot fast — within a day — so nobody keeps DMing a poster for a spot that's gone. In the Archive it stays on the record with strikethrough and a Filled tag.
To the Archive
The day after a gig's last date, it moves to the Archive, grouped by month, back to day 1. Nothing is deleted — Hot just stays short enough to actually use.
Range
San Diego and immediate surrounding areas, as far north as Anaheim. LA proper is a different ecosystem and stays off this list.
Read-only
This page never applies, comments, or messages anyone for you. You read it, you click through, you decide. That's deliberate for v1.
Feeding it
Got a new call, group, or poster? Send Noodle the screenshot or link as it comes in and it gets slotted in by gig date, and the past stuff rolls into the Archive.

Looking for an older call? Past gigs live in the Archive, grouped by month back to day 1 — Wed, Nov 11, 2026. Filled calls show there with strikethrough.
